## Privacy policy
> Source: https://sharpquiz.com/privacy-policy

### Introduction
The page title is "Privacy Policy for SHARP QUIZ". Sharp Quiz is described as a mobile application developed by Sharp Quiz ("we," "us," or "our"). The policy explains how they collect, use, disclose, and safeguard personal information when you use the Sharp Quiz app. Downloading, installing, or using the app means you agree to the policy. If you do not agree, do not use the app.
### Information the policy says may be collected
Information you provide: name, email address, and user-generated content such as quiz answers, comments, and feedback.
Usage information: how you use the app, including device type and operating system, app usage data such as quiz scores and preferences, and log information such as IP address and access times.
### How the policy says information is used
To deliver and maintain the app, personalize the experience, and improve the service.
To respond to inquiries, provide customer support, and send updates and notifications related to the app.
To analyze how users interact with the app and improve functionality.
### Disclosure
The policy says Sharp Quiz does not sell, rent, or trade personal information to third parties without consent. It may disclose information when required by law, court order, or government authority.
### Security and changes
The policy says they take reasonable measures to protect personal information, but no method of transmission or electronic storage is entirely secure, and they cannot guarantee absolute security.
They may update the policy and will notify users by posting the updated policy on the website or in the app. Continued use after changes is acknowledgment of the updated policy.

### What the listing says the app does
Sharp Quiz is a quiz app for students, programmers, and government exam aspirants. It offers MCQ practice, current affairs, and AI-powered features.
Programming and coding quizzes named on the listing: PHP, React and React Native, .NET and C#, Angular, Python, Java, C, C++, and more.
AI Topic Quiz Generator: enter a topic and the app generates quiz questions. The listing says this is for topic-wise revision, quick practice before exams, and learning without searching.
Document to Quiz Generator: upload a PDF (notes, books, study material) and the app creates quiz questions from it. The listing mentions exam notes, coaching material, and self-study PDFs.
Current affairs and daily quiz: national and international current affairs for UPSC, SSC, Banking, Railway, and State Government Exams, plus daily quizzes.
